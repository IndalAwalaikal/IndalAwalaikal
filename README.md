## 👋 A little about me

I’m **Indal Awalaikal**, an Informatics & Computer Engineering student at **Universitas Negeri Makassar**. I enjoy taking complicated ideas and turning them into products that are fast, clear, and maintainable.

My home base is backend engineering, but I like owning the full journey—from system design and data modeling to the interface people actually use. I’m also part of **COCONUT Computer Club**, contributing as an Executive Board member and R&D backend developer.
